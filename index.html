Vol Desk

An options and volatility analyst that prices, stress-tests, and explains with live market data.

What it does

Ask about any US stock, ETF, or index with listed options. Vol Desk pulls live quotes from Cboe, backs out implied volatility with its own Black-Scholes solver, and runs what-if scenarios on a position. Every number comes from a tool, never from the language model's memory, and each tool call is shown above the answer.

Try asking

Words you'll see

Implied vol
The market's forecast of how much a price will move over a year, read out of option prices.
Skew
How much more crash protection costs than upside bets, in vol points.
Delta, vega, theta
How much an option's price changes for a $1 stock move, a 1-point vol rise, and one day passing.
Delta hedge
Buying or selling stock so small moves in the price no longer change your P&L.

Quotes are about 15 minutes delayed. Black-Scholes treats options as European and ignores dividends; the risk-free rate is assumed at 4%. For learning and analysis, not investment advice.
